💡 Good To Know

Facts and news for your weekend at the circuit.

🏁

Silverstone returns

First ELMS round at Silverstone since 2020 — a six-year absence. Goodyear, the tyre supplier, is title sponsor of the round.

🔢

47 cars, four classes

LMP2, LMP2 Pro/Am, LMP3 and LMGT3. LMGT3 is the biggest class: 14 cars from six constructors.

🏆

Le Mans is the prize

Each class winner earns an automatic invitation to the 24 Hours of Le Mans. ELMS is the biggest feeder to the 24h.

⚡

Tightest race in years

With two rounds to go, all four championships are undecided. In LMGT3, only 13 points separate the top six crews.

🎯

Dillmann's pole

Tom Dillmann (#43) took the season's first pole for Inter Europol at 1:43.279. The Frenchman has deep F1 pedigree and knows every corner of Silverstone.

🇬🇧

Home advantage

Nick Yelloly (Inter Europol, LMP2) races on home soil. Last year his car finished second here. And TF Sport's Corvette is defended by British champions Eastwood, McDonald & Udell.

📐

How it works

Four-hour races, single tyre brand per class, and Le Mans-style driving substitutions. Points: 25/18/15/12/10/8/6/4/2/1 for the top ten, plus 1 for pole.

🏎️

Same spec, pure skill

LMP2 is a spec class — every car is an Oreca 07 with a Gibson V8, on Goodyears. That means the drivers and teams decide everything.

📰

WEC news

Robert Shwartzman (ex-F2 champion) has signed as a factory driver for Team Peugeot TotalEnergies in the 2027 FIA WEC, in the new Peugeot 9X8.

🎧

Watch free

All ELMS races are free to watch live on FIA WEC+, with replays and season highlights. Support acts: Michelin Le Mans Cup, Ligier European Series, Eurocup-3.

⏱️

0.141 seconds

Last year at Silverstone, reigning Pro/Am champions AO by TF (Hyett, Delétraz, Cameron) missed victory by just 0.141 seconds. They're 5th in the class now — hungry.

📅

One more to go

After today: Portimão, 10 October. A win today puts any championship leader a huge distance clear for the season decider.
